# Munch Privacy Policy

> **Template draft — have a licensed attorney review before public launch.**

_Last updated: 2026-06-12._

Munch ("Munch," "we," "us") is a swipe-based restaurant discovery app for
New York City and Boston. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why we
collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have
over your data. Munch is a discovery tool — not a restaurant or a delivery
service. By using Munch you agree to the practices described here.

## 1. What we collect

- **Account basics.** When you sign in with Apple, we receive the name you
  choose to share and an email address. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email"
  feature, that email is a private Apple relay address, not your real inbox.
- **Location.** With your permission, we use your precise or approximate
  location **only** to find restaurants near you. You can use approximate
  location, or deny location access and search by area instead.
- **Food preferences.** Cuisines, dietary tags, and similar preferences you
  set or that we infer from your activity.
- **Swipe and conversion history.** Which cards you swipe (and which way), and
  when you tap an outbound action such as "directions" or "order" (a
  "conversion"/"outbound tap").
- **A user identifier.** A device/authentication user id that ties your data
  to your account.
- **Usage analytics.** Basic, aggregate-style app usage and diagnostic
  information that helps us keep the app working and improve it.

We do **not** collect, scrape, or store data from Google Maps/Places, Yelp,
or Beli.

## 2. Why we collect it

| Data | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Account basics | Create and secure your account; contact you about your account. |
| Location | Find nearby restaurants. Used at search time; not sold. |
| Food preferences | Tailor the cards you see. |
| Swipe & conversion history | **(a)** Personalize your results, and **(b)** train and improve the recommendation model that powers Munch for all users. |
| User identifier | Associate your data with your account and sync across sessions. |
| Usage analytics | Keep the app stable, debug issues, and improve features. |

We want to be explicit: **your swipe and conversion history is used to train
our recommendation model.** This training improves recommendations for you and
for other Munch users. Match percentages shown in the app are derived from
Munch's own users — never from scraped third-party ratings.

## 3. Third parties we share with

- **Google Firebase** (Google LLC) — hosting, database (Firestore),
  authentication, and the Cloud Run service that runs our recommendation
  engine. Your account and activity data are stored in Firebase.
- **Anthropic** — generates the one-line "reason" text shown on a card. We
  send Anthropic only a **compact, non-identifying taste profile** (for
  example, your top cuisines and a mode/summary). We **never** send your name,
  email, or account identifier to Anthropic.
- **Deep-link handoffs.** When you tap "directions" or "order," Munch hands
  you off to **Apple Maps, DoorDash, or Uber Eats**. Once you leave Munch,
  that app's own privacy policy governs what it collects. Munch does not
  control those apps.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for
cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 4. Restaurant data sources & attribution

Restaurant information in Munch comes from open data sources, not from your
personal data:

- **OpenStreetMap** — base venue data. © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed
  under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Attribution and a link to
  <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright> are provided in the app and on our
  Data Sources page.
- **NYC Open Data** — DOHMH restaurant inspection data for New York City.
- **Analyze Boston** — food establishment license data for Boston, provided
  under the Public Domain Dedication and License (PDDL).

## 5. Retention & your right to deletion

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. You can
delete your account at any time:

- **In-app deletion.** Use the in-app "Delete account" option. This **purges
  all of your user data** from Munch.
- **Apple token revocation.** Because Munch uses Sign in with Apple, deleting
  your account also **revokes your Apple Sign in with Apple token** on Apple's
  side, as required by Apple (Apple Technical Note TN3194).

After deletion, some information may persist briefly in routine backups and
will age out, and we may retain limited records where the law requires.

## 6. Children's privacy / age

Munch is rated **13+** and is **not directed to children under 13**. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a
child under 13 has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

- **Know / access** the categories and specific pieces of personal information
  we have collected about you.
- **Delete** the personal information we hold about you (see Section 5).
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information.
- **Opt out of sale/sharing** — note that Munch does **not** sell or share your
  personal information as those terms are defined under the CPRA.
- **Limit use of sensitive personal information** (we use precise location only
  to find nearby restaurants).
- **Non-discrimination** for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise these rights, use in-app deletion or contact us (Section 9). We
will verify your request via your authenticated account.

## 8. Your rights in the EEA/UK (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the
right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your personal data, and to
object to certain processing. Our legal bases are:

- **Contract** — to provide the app and your account.
- **Consent** — for precise location access (which you can withdraw in your
  device settings at any time).
- **Legitimate interests** — to secure, debug, and improve the app and to train
  our recommendation model, balanced against your rights.

You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To
exercise your rights, use in-app deletion or contact us (Section 9).
