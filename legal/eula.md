# Munch EULA

> **Template draft — have a licensed attorney review before public launch.**

**Decision (Phase 0):** Munch relies on **Apple's standard Licensed
Application End User License Agreement** rather than a custom EULA — the
standard agreement is acceptable for this app's model (no unusual licensing
terms, no in-app purchases in v1).

Reference: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

If a custom EULA becomes necessary (e.g., user-generated content terms beyond
what the ToS covers), it replaces this file in Phase 6 and is declared in App
Store Connect.
