# Munch EULA

> **Template draft — have a licensed attorney review before public launch.**

_Last updated: 2026-06-12._

**Decision (confirmed Phase 6):** Munch relies on **Apple's standard Licensed
Application End User License Agreement** rather than a custom EULA. The standard
agreement is appropriate for this app's model — no unusual licensing terms and
no in-app purchases in v1.

Apple's standard EULA:
<https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/>

Your use of Munch is also subject to Munch's [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md)
and [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md), which govern Munch-specific matters
such as the discovery-tool disclaimers, sponsored placements, third-party
deep-link handoffs, and data practices.

If a custom EULA later becomes necessary (for example, user-generated content
terms beyond what the Terms of Service cover), it would replace this file and be
declared in App Store Connect.
