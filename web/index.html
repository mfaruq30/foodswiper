Munch.

Swipe to discover restaurants you'll actually love — in New York City and Boston.

Munch is a swipe-based restaurant discovery app. Tell us what you're into, swipe through nearby spots, and tap straight through to directions or ordering. Match percentages come from Munch's own community, not scraped ratings. Munch is a discovery tool — not the restaurant or the delivery service.
