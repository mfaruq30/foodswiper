Data Sources & Attribution

Munch's restaurant listings are built from open data. We credit every source as their licenses require. Match percentages shown in Munch come from Munch's own users — never from scraped third-party ratings.

OpenStreetMap

Base venue data is © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Full terms and attribution requirements: openstreetmap.org/copyright.

NYC Open Data

New York City restaurant data uses the DOHMH restaurant inspection dataset from NYC Open Data.

Provided by the City of New York via NYC Open Data.

Analyze Boston

Boston restaurant data uses food establishment license data from Analyze Boston.

Provided under the Public Domain Dedication and License (PDDL).

What we do not use

Munch does not collect, scrape, or share data from Google Maps/Places, Yelp, or Beli.
