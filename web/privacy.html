Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

Template draft — have a licensed attorney review before public launch.

Munch is a swipe-based restaurant discovery app for New York City and Boston. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights. Munch is a discovery tool — not a restaurant or a delivery service.

1. What we collect

  • Account basics — the name and email shared via Sign in with Apple. With Apple's "Hide My Email," that email is a private Apple relay address.
  • Location — precise or approximate, used only to find restaurants near you. You can use approximate location or deny access and search by area.
  • Food preferences — cuisines and dietary tags you set or that we infer.
  • Swipe & conversion history — which cards you swipe and when you tap an outbound action like "directions" or "order."
  • A user identifier — a device/auth user id tying your data to your account.
  • Usage analytics — basic app usage and diagnostics.

We do not collect, scrape, or store data from Google Maps/Places, Yelp, or Beli.

2. Why we collect it

To be explicit: your swipe and conversion history is used to train our recommendation model, improving results for you and for other users. Match percentages come from Munch's own users — never from scraped third-party ratings.

3. Third parties we share with

  • Google Firebase — hosting, database (Firestore), authentication, and the Cloud Run service that runs our recommendation engine.
  • Anthropic — generates the one-line "reason" on a card. We send only a compact, non-identifying taste profile (e.g. top cuisines and a mode). We never send your name, email, or account id to Anthropic.
  • Deep-link handoffs — tapping "directions" or "order" hands you off to Apple Maps, DoorDash, or Uber Eats. Once you leave Munch, that app's own privacy policy governs.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Restaurant data sources & attribution

Restaurant information comes from open data, not your personal data: OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0), NYC Open Data (DOHMH inspections), and Analyze Boston (food licenses, PDDL). See our Data Sources page for full attribution.

5. Retention & your right to deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete it anytime:

  • In-app deletion — the in-app "Delete account" option purges all of your user data from Munch.
  • Apple token revocation — because Munch uses Sign in with Apple, deletion also revokes your Apple token on Apple's side, as Apple requires (Technical Note TN3194).

Some data may persist briefly in routine backups and age out; we may retain limited records where the law requires.

6. Children's privacy / age

Munch is rated 13+ and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. California rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know/access, delete, and correct their personal information; to opt out of sale/sharing (Munch does not sell or share as defined by the CPRA); to limit use of sensitive personal information (we use precise location only to find nearby restaurants); and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. Use in-app deletion or contact us; we verify via your authenticated account.

8. EEA/UK rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your data, and to object to certain processing. Our legal bases are contract (providing the app), consent (precise location, withdrawable in device settings), and legitimate interests (securing, debugging, improving the app, and training our recommendation model). You may complain to your local data protection authority.
