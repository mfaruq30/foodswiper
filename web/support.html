Common questions

  • How do I delete my account and data? Open Munch, go to your account settings, and choose "Delete account." This purges all of your user data and, because Munch uses Sign in with Apple, also revokes your Apple sign-in token.
  • Why does Munch ask for my location? Only to find restaurants near you. You can use approximate location or search by area instead.
  • Where do restaurant listings come from? Open data — OpenStreetMap, NYC Open Data, and Analyze Boston. See Data Sources.
  • Is Munch the restaurant or the delivery service? No. Munch is a discovery tool. Orders, directions, pricing, and delivery happen in third-party apps like Apple Maps, DoorDash, or Uber Eats.
